# The firefly who was afraid of the dark

*A bedtime story — Book One*

---

Once upon a time, in a meadow at the foot of a hill, there was a little firefly called Nina.

Nina had a very strange problem for a firefly: she was afraid of the dark.

Every evening, when the sun went down behind the trees and the shadows stretched out across the grass, Nina hid under a clover leaf and shut her eyes.

«Don't go out, don't go out,» she whispered to herself.

Her sisters, meanwhile, flew happily over the meadow, switching their golden lanterns on and off. They looked like stars that had fallen out of the sky and decided to stay.

One night, while Nina was trembling under her leaf, she heard a small voice crying down by the brook.

It was a little cricket who had got lost.

«I can't find the way home,» the cricket sobbed. «It's too dark.»

Nina forgot her fear completely. She came out from under the leaf, and without even noticing, her lantern came on: a warm golden glow that lit up the grass, the pebbles and the frightened little cricket.

«Oh!» said the cricket, wiping his eyes. «Aren't you bright!»

Nina looked down and saw her own light reflected in the water of the brook. It was beautiful.

«Come on,» said Nina. «I'll take you.»

And so, flying very slowly, the firefly led the cricket across the meadow, past the sleeping flowers, all the way to his little house under the root of the old oak.

From that night on, Nina was never afraid of the dark again.

Because she had discovered a secret: the dark is never really dark, so long as somebody is willing to switch their own light on.

And now, my love, close your eyes. The fireflies are keeping watch over you.

Goodnight. 🌙✨
